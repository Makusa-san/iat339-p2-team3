News/Updates

From seasonal flavors to special events, stay up to date with the latest sweet stories from our maple kitchen

Introducing Our Spring Maple Collection

We’re excited to launch our new seasonal Maple Collection, inspired by the rich sweetness of Canadian maple syrup. From flaky maple croissants to our signature maple pecan danishes!

Freshly Baked Every Morning

Our ovens start early so your favorites are ready fresh every morning. Stop by to enjoy warm pastries, buttery layers, and the comforting aroma of maple in every bite. Quality and freshness are always our top priorities.

Now Serving Specialty Coffee

Great pastries deserve great coffee. We’ve partnered with a local roaster to bring you smooth, rich espresso and handcrafted lattes — the perfect pairing for your maple treat.

Holiday Pre-Orders Are Open

Planning a gathering? Our maple tarts, pastry boxes, and custom dessert trays are now available for pre-order. Let us make your celebrations sweeter!
